at its own route), and then the runner died: xcodebuild logged
`Restarting after unexpected exit, crash, or test timeout` and the xcresult records
**`Test crashed with signal term`**. No assertion failed; there is no `XCTAssert` line for this case

accessibility snapshotting ends the test runner. I did not re-derive that cause here — I am recording

was killed; it is not used as evidence either way.

Consequences for the claim, stated plainly:

* "twenty open/close cycles each render" is **not proven at `de59f71` by this run**. Three of the
  twenty were observed rendering before the runner died.
* The leak numbers (peak ≤ 4 WebContent, at rest 0) come from `scripts/t46_leak_probe.py` runs
  recorded in `docs/t46-av2-verdict.md` §1.2 and were **not** re-measured here.

Carded for the QA lane; this card re-takes verdicts and does not edit test code.
