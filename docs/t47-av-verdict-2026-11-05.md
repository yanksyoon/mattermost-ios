## 2. F1 — the DM case's last assertion is out of the harness's reach on this fixture

The test ends by finding the new conversation in the sidebar
(`T47CreateChannelUITests.swift:371-373`) through `revealSidebarRow`, which scrolls up to 25 times,
then down up to 25 times (`:474-489`). At the moment of the red, the app's own accessibility
description reports the sidebar's scroll bar as **`Vertical scroll bar, 29 pages, value: 0%`**, and
the rows it draws are `T8G probe 0071…0080`, `T8G probe 0061…0070`, `T8G probe 0051…0060`, … — i.e.
the list starts with custom-group content and the Direct-messages category is many pages below.

The fixture is the reason. `GET /api/v4/users/me/teams/ocz148uck3r88dcjikj497bosh/channels` for
`perfprobe` answers **410 channels**: 353 public, 55 direct, 1 group, 1 private. The conversation the
test opened (`kahpoaziefgr7xekdqsdyeddor`) **is** in that list, and the four assertions before the red
one passed:

1. the picker row for the teammate exists and was tapped (`:333-336`),
2. the pane opens titled with the person (`:340-341`),
3. the server's record of the pair is the same channel the pre-tap check named, type `D`, with both
   members (`:353-368`),
4. only then the sidebar assertion fails (`:372`).

So the red is "the reveal loop cannot cross 29 pages", not "the app failed to list the conversation".
It cannot be closed as green here, and it cannot be reported as a product defect either: the test
throws away the distinction at that one assertion. Filed as a card for the QA lane (see §5).

This supersedes the old verdict's finding (the `collectionViews` reveal), which `e2e3802` repaired:
`revealSidebarRow` now scrolls `channelSidebar.scroll` and leaves its swipe counts in an attachment.
